How to Order

Follow these steps to place your order with M. Yousaf Cloth House.

  1. Select the products you want and fill in the order form below.
  2. Enter your correct name, phone number and complete Sialkot delivery address.
  3. We will confirm product availability and the product price with you.
  4. Once the final amount is confirmed, transfer payment to the Meezan Bank account shown below.
  5. Send your payment screenshot through WhatsApp. Your payment must be verified before the order is processed.
